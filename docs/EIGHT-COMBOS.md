# Eight corporate gifting combos

The Diwali & Hampers collection now contains eight combos: the four original sets plus Executive Diary & Pen Gift Set, Coffee & Planning Gift Box, Smart Desk Corporate Gift Set, and Everyday Essentials Gift Hamper. Total catalog: 22 products. The header's Enterprise Services item is now Corporate Gifts and links to /corporate-gifts on desktop and mobile.

Four new representative images were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Exact prompts and original source paths are in scripts/additional-combos.json. Final WebP assets are in public/images/products/corporate-gift-combos. They retain the existing AI-generated image disclosure on product detail pages.

The backed-up, repeatable database update is scripts/expand-gift-combos.ts. Validation passed: TypeScript, ESLint, 22 product pages/enquiries, eight category filters, 37 image URLs and 127 redirects. The collection was also checked in the browser; screenshot: docs/diwali-eight-combos-preview.png.
